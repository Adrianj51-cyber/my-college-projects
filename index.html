Assignment 4: Hardware Evaluation

Related Chapter: Chapter 3 – Evaluating Hardware

Introduction

Computer hardware is made up of physical parts that work together to make a computer function. Each component has a different job, and choosing the right hardware can affect a computer's speed, performance, storage, and overall experience. This assignment evaluates four important hardware components: the CPU, RAM, SSD, and GPU.

Hardware Comparison Table

The table explains what each component does and why it is important.

Component Description Importance
CPU The Central Processing Unit processes instructions and performs calculations. It controls many computer operations and affects overall performance.
RAM Random Access Memory temporarily stores information that programs are currently using. More RAM helps a computer run multiple programs and tasks smoothly.
SSD A Solid State Drive stores files, programs, and the operating system using flash memory. It helps a computer start faster and load files and applications more quickly.
GPU The Graphics Processing Unit handles graphics, images, videos, and visual calculations. It is important for gaming, video editing, graphic design, and other demanding visual tasks.

Hardware Components Explained

1. CPU – Processor

The CPU is often called the brain of the computer. It handles instructions from programs and performs calculations. A faster processor can help a computer complete tasks more efficiently.

2. RAM – Memory

RAM temporarily holds information that the computer needs right away. It allows users to switch between applications and keep multiple tasks running. RAM does not permanently save information when the computer is turned off.

3. SSD – Storage

An SSD saves files, applications, and the operating system. Unlike older hard drives, SSDs have no moving parts and can access stored information quickly. This makes computers faster when starting up and opening programs.

4. GPU – Graphics Card

The GPU processes graphics and visual information. It is especially useful for gaming, video editing, animation, and other tasks that require detailed images and video.

Conclusion

All four hardware components play an important role in a computer. The CPU processes instructions, RAM helps programs run smoothly, the SSD stores information, and the GPU handles graphics. When evaluating a computer, it is important to understand what each component does and how it affects the user's needs. Choosing the right combination of hardware can improve performance and make a computer more useful for school, work, gaming, and creative projects.

Homework 4: AI Movie Agent

1. Project Overview

The goal of this project is to create an AI Agent workflow that takes a movie idea and turns it into a short film. The workflow uses multiple stages, including planning, writing, character development, storyboarding, video generation, audio, and editing.

My movie idea is a short inspirational sports drama about a young football player who faces challenges but learns that hard work, teamwork, and determination can help him reach his goals.

Movie Title: One More Play

Genre: Sports drama / Inspirational

Target Audience: Teenagers, students, athletes, and sports fans

Planned Length: Approximately 3–5 minutes

2. AI Agent Workflow

Each agent has a specific responsibility. The agents pass their results to the next stage so the project can move from an idea to a finished movie.

1. Director Agent: Receives the movie idea and decides the genre, audience, tone, and direction.
2. Scriptwriting Agent: Creates the title, story, characters, scenes, dialogue, and ending.
3. Character Agent: Develops the characters and keeps their appearance consistent.
4. Storyboard Agent: Breaks the script into scenes and plans camera angles, lighting, and action.
5. Video Generation Agent: Turns scene descriptions into video clips using an AI video-generation platform.
6. Audio Agent: Plans voices, music, sound effects, and background sounds.
7. Editing Agent: Organizes clips, dialogue, music, transitions, and credits into the final movie.

3. Movie Script

Title: One More Play

Story Summary:

A high school football player named Marcus is struggling with pressure, injuries, and doubts about his future. After a difficult practice, he considers giving up. His coach reminds him that success is not about never falling down. It is about choosing to get back up. Marcus returns to practice, works with his teammates, and eventually gets another opportunity to make a difference for his team.

Main Characters

  • Marcus: A determined young football player who wants to prove himself.
  • Coach Daniels: A supportive coach who teaches Marcus about discipline and perseverance.
  • Jordan: Marcus's teammate and close friend who encourages him not to quit.

Scene 1 – The Pressure

Marcus sits alone in the locker room after a difficult practice. He looks down at his football equipment and wonders if all the hard work is worth it.

Marcus: "I don't know if I can keep doing this."

Scene 2 – The Conversation

Coach Daniels walks into the locker room and notices Marcus sitting by himself. He sits next to him and reminds him why he started playing.

Coach Daniels: "You think being strong means never struggling. It doesn't. It means getting back up when things get difficult."

Scene 3 – The Work

Marcus returns to practice. He runs drills, works with his teammates, and pushes through difficult moments. Jordan encourages him during the workout.

Jordan: "Come on, we've got one more rep!"

Marcus: "Let's get it done."

Scene 4 – One More Play

During an important game, Marcus gets another opportunity. The team lines up, the ball is snapped, and Marcus helps make a crucial block that allows his teammate to score.

Coach Daniels: "That's what happens when you don't give up!"

Ending

Marcus celebrates with his teammates. The movie ends with a message: "Your biggest victories start when you decide not to quit."
